MOTHER OF ALL ASIA – TOWER OF PEACE

The Mother of All Asia – Tower of Peace is a monument dedicated to the Blessed Virgin Mary, symbolizing peace and unity throughout Asia and the world. Standing at 98.15 meters (322 feet) from the ground to the top of the cross on Mary’s crown, it is recognized as the tallest statue of Mary in the world. Its height is equivalent to a 33-story building, representing the 33 years of Jesus Christ’s life on Earth. The monument is also ranked as the 9th tallest statue globally, standing taller than the Statue of Liberty in New York and Christ the Redeemer in Rio de Janeiro.

Aside from its impressive height, the monument is considered the world’s only livable statue, with an estimated floor area of 12,353 square meters or around 1.2353 hectares. The crown is adorned with 12 stars, symbolizing the 12 apostles of Jesus, while the structure itself is supported by 12 foundations representing the 12 tribes of Israel. Visitors can reach the crown either by climbing 420 steps or by using one of the monument’s three elevators.

The hands and face of Mary were created by the late sculptor and National Artist Ed Castrillo, who began the project in 2008. After these parts were completed in 2010, they were temporarily displayed until 2017 before being incorporated into the final structure. Construction of the Tower of Peace started in 2013 and was completed in 2021, in time for the Philippines’ celebration of 500 years of Christianity. The title “Mother of All Asia” was inspired by a prayer of Pope Benedict XVI honoring Our Lady of Sheshan as Mother of the Church in China and all of Asia.

HISTORY OF THE MONTEMARIA INTERNATIONAL PILGRIMAGE & CONFERENCE CENTER

Located at the center of a 130-hectare township project is the Montemaria International Pilgrimage & Conference Center, a faith-centered tourism destination and legacy project initiated by Batangas Governor Dodo Mandanas, who also serves as the founding chairman of Montemaria Asia Pilgrims Inc.

Founded on February 8, 2013, Montemaria Asia Pilgrims, Inc. (MAPI) is a stock, non-profit membership organization officially registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission on April 12, 2013. The organization owns and develops the 8-hectare tourism property.

Its main attraction is the Mother of All Asia – Tower of Peace, built on the highest point of the plateau at an elevation of 148 meters above sea level. The monument overlooks the Verde Island Passage, known as the center of marine biodiversity in the world, while symbolically welcoming both local and international vessels entering the peaceful waters of Batangas Bay.

The long-term vision of the Montemaria International Pilgrimage & Conference Center is to establish a walkable community featuring covered pathways that connect buildings and theme park attractions, along with landscaped gardens, waterways, and various species of shade and fruit-bearing trees.
